# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive executive dashboard built in Power BI to analyze workforce dynamics, track employee turnover, and explore demographic and compensation metrics.

---

## 📸 Dashboard Overview

![HR Analytics Dashboard](HR%20ANALYTICS%20DASHBOARD.png)

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Metrics

* **Executive KPI Cards**:
  * **Total Employees**: 1,480
  * **Active Workforce**: 1,239
  * **Attrition Count**: 241
  * **Attrition Rate**: 16.3%
  * **Average Age**: 37.57 Years
  * **Average Experience**: 7.38 Years
* **Departmental Attrition**: Visualizing turnover distribution across Sales, Operations, HR, IT, Marketing, and Finance.
* **Salary & Satisfaction Breakdown**: Cross-analyzing turnover rates across salary slabs (0–3 LPA, 3–6 LPA, 6–10 LPA, 10+ LPA) and job role satisfaction levels.
* **Demographics & Experience Trends**:
  * Age Group Distributions (18–25, 26–35, 36–45, 46–55, 55+)
  * Attrition by Gender
  * Working Experience Years Trend
  * Department Employee Count Funnel Chart

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Power BI Desktop**: Custom canvas design, KPI visuals, and interactive reporting.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**: Dynamic measures for headcount, attrition rate, and averages.
* **Power Query**: Data cleaning and structure transformations.
